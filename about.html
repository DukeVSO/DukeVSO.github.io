Most IT problems don't announce themselves. They quietly pile up; slow systems, phishing emails that slip through, staff who don't know what to click and what to avoid; until one day, something breaks badly.

I help organisations fix that.

I'm Victor Omotayo, an IT support and cybersecurity specialist based in Nigeria, available for remote roles globally. With a BSc in Computer Science and hands-on experience across IT support, cybersecurity, and system administration, I bring both the technical skills and the field-tested instincts that come from working in high-pressure environments.

At the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), I delivered IT support and data management for one of Nigeria's most critical national operations; where accuracy and reliability weren't optional. As a secondary school ICT teacher, I translated complex cybersecurity concepts into practical awareness for students, because the best defence against threats is an informed team.

I hold a Certified Phishing Prevention Specialist (CPPS) certification and have completed training with ISC2, Forward Edge Consulting, and TS Academy; not to collect badges, but to stay sharp on how real threats evolve and how to stop them.

Beyond the technical, I bring strong written communication and administrative support skills; useful when your IT work involves documentation, reporting, or coordinating across teams.
